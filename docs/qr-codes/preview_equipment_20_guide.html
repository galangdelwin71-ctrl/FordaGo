SELECT WORKOUT EXERCISE:
Cable Rope Tricep Pushdown BEGINNER
PRIMARY TARGET: Triceps Brachii (Lateral & Long Head)
SECONDARY / STABILIZERS:
Forearm Brachioradialis Core
🔥 Recommended Protocol: 3-4 Sets · 12-15 Reps
Step-by-Step Form & Execution
1 Set pulley to highest notch and attach double-rope attachment.
2 Grip ropes with neutral grip and tuck elbows tightly against ribcage.
3 Push rope straight down, spreading ends apart at bottom for maximum squeeze.
4 Control return to a 90-degree elbow bend over 2 seconds.
