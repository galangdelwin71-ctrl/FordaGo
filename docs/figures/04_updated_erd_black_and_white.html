Relational Entity-Relationship Diagram (ERD) Architectural Rationale:
The FordaGO Entity-Relationship Diagram models the complete relational schema operating in 3NF compliance. The users table serves as the primary relational root with role-based attributes segregating members, trainers, employees, and administrators. Referential integrity is strictly maintained via foreign keys (FK) enforcing cascading policies on attendance check-ins, personal strength milestones, workout split logs, and POS cart orders. The conversations, messages, and workout_plan_proposals entities form an isolated real-time communications relational cluster, enabling coach-client consultation and atomic proposal dispatch. All connector lines utilize Crow's foot cardinality notation entering target entity borders perpendicularly.
