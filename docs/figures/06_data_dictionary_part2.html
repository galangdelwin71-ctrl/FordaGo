Data Schema & Integrity Constraints Narrative (Part 2):
Part 2 details the interactive communication, workout proposal, and personal performance tracking entities. The conversations and messages tables underpin the real-time chat architecture powered by Laravel Reverb WebSockets. Custom workout offers created by certified coaches are codified in workout_plan_proposals with JSON exercise structures. Upon client acceptance, proposals instantiate executable workout logs in workout_sessions, linking directly to member strength progressions recorded in personal_records.
