Methodological Explanation of the Project Development Schedule:
The project development timeline spans nine (9) academic months (January through September). The conceptualization, requirements gathering, and architectural modeling phases were executed in Q1 (January–March). System construction, encompassing the Laravel 11 backend, Ionic 8/Angular 18 mobile frontend, and real-time Reverb WebSocket subsystems, was completed from April to July. During August and September, developmental focus transitions strictly to Full System Testing, On-Site Pilot Deployment at AFFORDA Gym (Cabiao Branch), and Software Quality Evaluation under the ISO/IEC 25010 standard involving 15 purposively sampled IT experts, gym administrators, coaches, and members. The final weeks of September are reserved for statistical data aggregation (weighted mean analysis) and the culminating Capstone Oral Defense.
