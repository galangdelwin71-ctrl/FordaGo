Data Schema & Integrity Constraints Narrative (Part 1):
Part 1 of the Data Dictionary defines the foundational user, attendance, and fitness trainer tables. The users table establishes role-based identity across five distinct system roles (member, coach, employee, admin, super_admin) and manages pass lifecycle states. The attendance entity logs real-time turnstile check-in timestamps, pass types, and staff confirmations with foreign key linkage to users.id. Trainer profiles, duty availability schedules, and packaged training programs are modeled across coach_profiles, coach_availability, and coach_programs to maintain referential integrity and support on-demand booking.
