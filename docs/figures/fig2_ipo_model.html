FORDAGO: MOBILE-BASED GYM DATABASE
MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

for AFFORDA Gym – Cabiao Branch

INPUT

(System Data, Assets & Specifications)
•
Member & Account Information User profiles, membership pass tiers, encrypted login credentials, dynamic QR entrance pass tokens, and biometric parameters.
•
Physical QR Placards & Telemetry Gym entrance check-in QR placards, machine equipment QR placards, and mobile device camera optical video streams.
•
Operational POS & Inventory Data Supplement retail catalog, stock quantities, unit costs, pricing matrices, walk-in cash payments, and PayMongo transaction receipts.
•
Coaching & Training Records Trainer profiles, bespoke workout plan proposals, consultation chat feeds, daily split routines, and 1RM personal benchmarks.
•
Evaluation Metrics & Standards ISO/IEC 25010 software quality characteristics, Likert scale evaluation forms, and empirical usability assessment questionnaires.

PROCESS

(Iterative Development & Engineering)
•
Planning & Feasibility Analysis Investigating gym bottlenecks, stakeholder interviews, operational delay audits, risk mitigation plans, and agile work breakdown.
•
Architecture & 3NF Database Design Formulating Use Case diagrams, Context Diagram Level 0, DFD Level 1, and 3NF normalized schema across users, attendances, and orders.
•
Full-Stack Application Development Building Laravel 13 REST API, Ionic 8/Angular standalone mobile client, Reverb WebSocket channels, and mobile QR entrance verification logic.
•
Multi-Channel Payment Integration Connecting PayMongo gateway for online GCash, Maya, and card transactions, alongside counter POS cash register operations.
•
Verification & Quality Assessment Unit and functional testing, pilot deployment at AFFORDA Gym, and empirical ISO/IEC 25010 evaluation with statistical analysis.

OUTPUT

(Deployed System & Strategic Deliverables)
•
FordaGO Mobile Application Deployed cross-platform app providing dynamic QR entry passes, workout logging, PR benchmarks, and supplement cart checkout.
•
Central Administrative Web Portal Management console for walk-in member onboarding, pass renewals, POS counter sales, inventory tracking, and vector PDF/Excel reports.
•
Coach Studio & Consultation Suite Dedicated trainer web workspace managing trainee client rosters, custom workout proposals, and real-time consultation messaging.
•
Mobile QR Attendance & Front-Desk Live Roster Member mobile camera QR check-in confirming attendance entries, verifying active pass validity, preventing pass-sharing, and broadcasting live alerts to front-desk staff.
•
ISO/IEC 25010 Quality Report Validated assessment findings establishing high software quality, system reliability, operational efficiency, and user satisfaction.
