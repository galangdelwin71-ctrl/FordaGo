Conceptual Framework (IPO Model)

FordaGO: Mobile-Based Gym Database Management System

Input

System Data, Requirements, & Evaluation Criteria
User & Account Profiles:
•
5-Tier Authenticated Roles: Super Admin, Gym Admin, Employee (Front-Desk), Coach, and Member credentials & profiles.
Gym Operations & Business Rules:
•
Membership Passes: Tier durations (Daily, 30-Day, 90-Day, Annual), rates, and active pass policies.
•
Equipment Catalog: Machine names, specs, targeted muscle groups, and movement video URLs.
Optical Hardware & Sensor Inputs:
•
QR Optical Telemetry: Smartphone camera video stream for QR attendance & equipment placard scanning.
Fitness & Consultation Data:
•
Workouts & Benchmarks: Personal Record (PR) lifting metrics and customized weekly split routines.
•
Trainer Availability & Chat: Coach working slots, duplex messages, and structured workout proposals.
Inventory & Point-of-Sale Data:
•
Supplements & Transactions: Product stock counts, cart orders, and GCash reference numbers/receipts.
Evaluation Parameters:
•
ISO/IEC 25010 Quality Model: 8 software criteria evaluated by 10 Purposively Sampled Respondents (5 IT Experts and 5 Active Gym Members) via 4-Point Likert Scale.

Process

System Engineering, Architecture, & Evaluation Phases
1.
Requirements Engineering: Stakeholder interviews at AFFORDA Gym, bottleneck analysis, and Agile SDLC planning.
2.
Architectural Modeling: Decoupled Client-Server framework, 5-actor Use Case diagram, Context Diagram, and DFD Level 1.
3.
Database 3NF Normalization: Eliminating data redundancy and anomalies into normalized relational tables (MySQL 8.0).
4.
Backend REST API Engineering: Laravel 11 (PHP 8.2+) controllers, Eloquent ORM, Sanctum token authentication, & 5-tier RBAC.
5.
Real-Time WebSocket Server: Laravel Reverb server (Port 8080) for high-speed duplex chat and live proposal alerts.
6.
Mobile Client Construction: Cross-platform Ionic 8 + Angular 18 client with Capacitor camera barcode scanner plugins.
7.
Turnstile & Attendance Logic: Dynamic QR verification with debounced anti-pass-sharing timestamp guards.
8.
POS & Atomic Deductions: ACID inventory stock deductions and multi-item GCash payment proof auditing.
9.
Dynamic Report Generation: jsPDF & AutoTable client engines rendering vector PDF revenue summaries and Excel logs.
10.
Cloud VPS Deployment & Testing: Podman container stack, automated 3-hour backup tasks, and ISO/IEC 25010 survey analysis.

Output

Operational Platforms, Records, Reports, & Evaluation
Deployed Client Platforms:
•
Member Mobile Portal: Cross-platform mobile app for workout, attendance, tutorial, and shop access.
•
Coach Studio Hub: Workspace for trainee management, duplex messaging, and in-chat proposals.
•
Admin Command Center: Multi-tier web/terminal hub for Super Admin, Admin, and Front-Desk staff.
Operational Records & Automation:
•
Digital Turnstile Attendance Logs: Automated entrance traffic logs with pass expiration auditing.
•
Machine Movement Tutorials: On-demand optical equipment guides and muscle engagement animations.
•
PR & Workout Progression: Benchmark lifting records, strength histories, and custom split routines.
•
POS Orders & Sales Records: Point-of-sale inventory transaction receipts and GCash approval logs.
Management Analytics & System Evaluation:
•
Dynamic Vector PDF/Excel Reports: Attendance, revenue, and inventory analytics ready for export.
•
Empirical Quality Report: Highly acceptable ISO/IEC 25010 rating (3.75 IT Experts, 3.82 Active Gym Members).
