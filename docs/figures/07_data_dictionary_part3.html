Data Schema & Integrity Constraints Narrative (Part 3):
Part 3 outlines the e-commerce inventory, QR equipment guide telemetry, and feedback mechanisms. The products and orders tables enforce atomic inventory deductions using database row-level locking (lockForUpdate()) during checkout to prevent race conditions. The equipment and equipment_scan_logs tables link physical QR placards on gym machines with digital tutorial instructions and track scan analytics. Finally, notifications and feedbacks deliver automated administrative alerts and user satisfaction metrics.
