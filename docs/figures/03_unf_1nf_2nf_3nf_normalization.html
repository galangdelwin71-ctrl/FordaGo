Relational Database Normalization Rationale:
Database normalization was systematically executed from Unnormalized Form (UNF) through Third Normal Form (3NF) to guarantee relational integrity, optimal query performance, and the total prevention of insertion, update, and deletion anomalies. In 1NF, repeating multi-valued arrays were dismantled into atomic attributes. In 2NF, partial functional dependencies were resolved by creating dedicated relational entities for coaching packages, chat channels, and program bookings. In 3NF, transitive dependencies were fully decoupled (e.g., separating product catalogue prices from POS order audit logs and segregating QR scan telemetry). As a result, the FordaGO relational schema operates in complete 3NF compliance.
