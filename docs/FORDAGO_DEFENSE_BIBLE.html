1. Core System Overview

FordaGO is an automated gym management, real-time coaching, and optical turnstile QR access system built specifically for AFFORDA Gym in Cabiao, Nueva Ecija. It eliminates vulnerable manual paper logbooks through an optical turnstile check-in mechanism, provides machine-mounted exercise guides for lifters, empowers accredited coaches with in-chat workout proposal tools, and delivers automated point-of-sale inventory and financial reporting.

3. Critical Technical Defense Answers (Architecture & Design Decisions)

Q: Why use over-the-counter Cash and GCash reference verification instead of third-party payment gateways (e.g., PayMongo or Stripe)?
Technical Defense: AFFORDA Gym is a local community gym in Cabiao, Nueva Ecija. Commercial payment gateways impose 2.5% transaction processing surcharges, require merchant corporate bank credentials (DTI/SEC), and introduce payment failure risks during local connectivity hiccups. Counter Cash and manual GCash reference audits ensure zero fee deductions, allowing 100% of revenue to reach gym operations while maintaining a fully digital audit trail.
Q: Why use mobile camera QR code scanning instead of physical RFID cards or Fingerprint Biometrics?
Technical Defense: Physical RFID cards are easily lost, damaged, forgotten, or shared, and incur recurring card manufacturing costs for gym owners. Optical fingerprint scanners in fitness environments suffer from sweat and chalk buildup, leading to false rejection rates and hygiene concerns. Dynamic optical QR codes rendered directly on members' personal smartphones eliminate physical contact (100% hygienic), require zero hardware badge replacement costs, and are always available on the lifter's device.
Q: How does the system prevent negative inventory when multiple customers buy the last supplement concurrently?
Technical Defense: We implemented ACID database transactions combined with Pessimistic Row-Level Locking (lockForUpdate() in Laravel/MySQL InnoDB). The database places an exclusive write lock on the target product row during the initial transaction. Any concurrent checkout thread is queued until the first transaction commits. When stock drops to zero, subsequent attempts are rejected with an "Out of Stock" notification, mathematically preventing negative inventory.
Q: How does the system prevent members from sharing their QR pass with non-members (Anti-Pass-Sharing)?
Technical Defense: The backend implements an automated Anti-Pass-Sharing validation algorithm. Upon each scan, the server queries the member's most recent check-in timestamp. If the QR pass was already scanned within the configurable cooldown interval, the turnstile scanner flags the attempt as a duplicate entry violation, denies access, and triggers an immediate visual alert on the receptionist terminal.
Q: Why does GitHub indicate high TypeScript percentage (~82%) compared to PHP (~4%)?
Technical Defense: In Angular 18, every screen across 30+ pages consists of dedicated TypeScript files for component logic, dependency injection services, route guards, and data interfaces. Conversely, Laravel framework vendor dependencies are excluded from git via .gitignore. The PHP codebase consists strictly of high-density custom business logic, 21 controllers, and over 120+ REST API endpoints.
